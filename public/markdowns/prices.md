# Prices

Deposplit can be used free of charge to share up to three secrets at a time — discarding one frees its slot straight away. There are neither [subscriptions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wko-2c7lZL8) nor ads – nevertheless, [you are not the product](https://www.wisconsin.edu/information-security/2026/05/22/if-its-free-youre-the-product-free-stuff-scam-awareness/).

Deposplit's business model is freemium. Deposplit (or rather Squeng AG) makes money with one-time in-app purchases that allow for sharing more than three secrets and that activate BYOR (*bring your own relay*).
