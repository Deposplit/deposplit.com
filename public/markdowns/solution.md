## The Solution

Your secret is split and shared with *n* (e.g., 7) persons of your choice such that only *k* (e.g., 4) splits/shares are necessary to recover it. Unless *k* or more (4, 5, 6, or 7 in our example) persons collude, **no person learns anything** about your secret. And up to *n - k* (3 in our example) persons could lose the split/share entrusted to them without your secret being lost.
