# Support

Questions, problems or suggestions? Write to [deposplit@squeng.com](mailto:deposplit@squeng.com),
in English or German.

A report is easiest to follow up on when it names the phone, the version of Android or iOS, and
the version of Deposplit.

**Never send a secret or a share.** Squeng AG does not need either and could not recover a secret
anyway: secrets are split and encrypted on the phone, and the relay passes on only what it cannot
read. A secret can be reconstructed only from enough of its shares, and those are held by the
contacts it was shared with.
